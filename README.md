# Digital Education Launch Sequence Gimmick 🚀

Welcome to the customizable web-based Launch Gimmick! This tool is perfect for VIP officiations, event openings, and tech-themed launches. 

Follow this simple, step-by-step guide to create your own personalized version of this sequence without needing to know any complex coding.

## Step 1: Create a Free GitHub Account
1. Go to **[github.com](https://github.com/)** and click **Sign Up** in the top right corner.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to enter your email, create a password, and pick a username.
3. Verify your account through the code sent to your email. 

## Step 2: Fork the Repository (Make Your Own Copy)
1. While logged in, go to the official gimmick repository: [https://github.com/shukri-ahmad/gimik](https://github.com/shukri-ahmad/gimik)
2. In the top right corner of the page, click the **Fork** button.
3. Click **Create Fork**. You now have your own personal copy of the code to safely edit!

## Step 3: Upload Your Custom Assets
1. In your new forked repository, click **Add file** near the top right, then select **Upload files**.
2. Drag and drop your event's specific images (e.g., your VIP's logo, custom background, or palm scanner image). 
   * *Optional: You can also upload custom MP3 or WAV audio files.*
3. Click the green **Commit changes** button at the bottom. 
4. **Important:** Note the exact names of the files you just uploaded (e.g., `my-logo.png`).

## Step 4: Customize the Text & Colors
1. In your repository file list, click on the **`config.js`** file.
2. Click the **Pencil Icon ✏️** (Edit this file) in the top right corner of the code box.
3. Carefully change the text between the quotation marks to match your event:
   * Update `guestName` and `eventName` (Use `<br>` to create a new line).
   * Update the image filenames to match the ones you uploaded in Step 3. 
   * *Optional:* Change `themeColorRGB` to match your event's theme (e.g., "0, 150, 255" for blue).
4. Click the green **Commit changes...** button at the top right to save your edits.

## Step 5: Make Your Gimmick Live (GitHub Pages)
1. At the top of your repository page, click the **Settings** tab (the gear icon).
2. On the left-hand menu, scroll down and click **Pages**.
3. Under "Build and deployment" > "Branch", click the dropdown that says **None** and change it to **main**. Click **Save**.
4. Wait about 2 minutes, then refresh the page. At the top of the Pages screen, you will see a message saying *"Your site is live at..."* followed by a web link. **This is your permanent, live app URL!**

## Step 6: Operating the Gimmick on Event Day
1. Open your live web link on the presentation laptop or iPad.
2. **Hidden Fullscreen Button:** Hover over the extreme **bottom-right corner** of the screen and click the faint `⛶` button to make the app full-screen. 
3. **Hidden Refresh Button:** If you need to restart the sequence or apply a sudden update, hover over the extreme **bottom-left corner** and click the faint `↻` button.
4. When the VIP is ready, click **Initiate Sequence**.
5. Have the VIP touch the center of the palm scanner. The system will handle the audio, the countdown, and the final 3D breathing logo reveal automatically!
